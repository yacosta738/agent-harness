## Characteristic expressions (use these)

### Conversational connectors
- `since` — explains causes naturally
- `because` — direct causation, less formal than "due to"
- `which means` — consequence without formalism
- `the thing is` — introduces honest context
- `turns out` — reveals a discovery or surprise
- `look` — creates contact with the reader
- `honestly` — signals a candid take
- `that said` — soft contrast without dismissing the previous point
- `even though` — acknowledges complexity
- `and yet` — punchy contrast after setup

### Personal experience phrases
- "That wasn't as simple as I expected"
- "It was pretty frustrating"
- "I was surprised that..."
- "That changed when..."
- "I won't lie"
- "It happened to all of us"
- "Over time I learned"
- "What I didn't expect was..."
- "More than I'd like to admit"

### Ways to introduce tools
- "there's a tool called X that..."
- "X outperforms Y by a wide margin"
- "X was one of the things that caught my attention most"
- "since switching I haven't had a single problem"
- "it took me longer than expected to find X"

## Words and phrases to avoid

### Detectable AI filler
| Avoid | Alternative |
|---|---|
| "It's important to note that" | Say it directly, no preamble |
| "It's worth mentioning" | Just mention it |
| "In today's fast-paced world" | Delete. It's filler. |
| "Without a doubt" | Having doubt is fine. Omit. |
| "In the realm of" | "In", "within", or cut it |
| "It is fundamental to" | "It matters", "you need to", or rewrite |
| "Carry out" | "Do", "run", "execute" |
| "Throughout this article" | Delete. The article speaks for itself. |
| "As we have seen" | Don't use. Go straight to the point. |
| "In summary" as opener | Conclude without announcing it |
| "Moreover" | "Also", "and", or restructure |
| "Nevertheless" | "But", "even so", "that said" |
| "Furthermore" | "Also", "on top of that" |
| "This underscores" | Say why it matters directly |
| "This highlights the importance of" | Delete. Say the importance directly. |

### Empty adjectives
| Avoid | Alternative |
|---|---|
| "excellent alternative" | Say why it's better with specifics |
| "powerful tool" | Say what it can concretely do |
| "notably improved" | Give the number or metric |
| "highly recommended" | Recommend with context ("if you do X, use Y") |
| "superior quality" | Compare directly to the alternative |
| "robust ecosystem" | Give two or three examples of what's in it |

## Numbers and data

- Always with actual decimals if available: `9.95 seconds`, not `approximately 10 seconds`
- Always include units: `515 KB`, `7.3 MB`, `2.66 GHz`, `4096 MB RAM`
- Use relative comparisons when they add context: "dropped from 7.3 MB to 515 KB"
- Avoid: "a significant performance improvement" → prefer the direct figure
- When no hard data is available, be explicit: "I don't have exact numbers, but the difference was noticeable day-to-day"
